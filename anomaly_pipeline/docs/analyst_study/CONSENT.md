# Participant information and consent

**Study:** Explanations for network anomaly alerts (MSc thesis, DTU Compute, in collaboration with Terma A/S)
**Researcher:** Mounika Maidamshetti (s250148). **Supervisors:** Dr. Gaurav Choudhary (DTU), Mathias Højte Olsen (Terma).

**What you will do.** You will review 20 alerts from an anomaly detector that ran on a public benchmark dataset
(no Terma data, no real users' data). For each alert you decide whether it is an attack and what kind, and rate the
explanation when one is shown. It takes about 45 minutes.

**What is recorded.** Your answers, the time per alert, your job role and years of experience, and any comments.
No name, e-mail or other identifying information is stored with your answers; you get a participant ID (P1, P2, ...).
Results are reported only in aggregate or by participant ID in the thesis.

**Your rights.** Participation is voluntary. You can stop at any time without giving a reason, and you can ask for your
answers to be deleted until the thesis is submitted. This is a test of the explanations, not of you.
