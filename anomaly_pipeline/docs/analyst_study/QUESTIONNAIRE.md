Rate 1 (strongly disagree) to 5 (strongly agree). Items adapted from the Explanation Satisfaction Scale
(Hoffman, Mueller, Klein and Litman, 2018, "Metrics for Explainable AI: Challenges and Prospects").

| # | Statement | 1-5 |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | The explanations helped me understand why the detector raised an alert. | |

| 4 | The explanations told me what to check next. | |
| 5 | I could tell when the detector was likely wrong. | |
| 6 | The comparison with "normal for this service role" was useful. | |

Open questions:
